The concept

Fly to the moon. Bring your friends.

Growing up means leaving home for your own adventure, and every mile puts distance between you and the friends who knew you first. The app already tells this story: the brush timer is a moon that fills from new to full in two minutes while a toothbrush rocket orbits it. The video builds on that.

Beat 8 · 1:40–2:00 · the money shot

Three skies, one moon

First the real app footage: record all three phones at the same moment so the moons fill in sync, with each showing the other two friends' avatars. Then a motion graphic slides the three moons together into one moon with three rockets orbiting it. Keep that part clearly stylised, because the app itself shows friends as avatars.
