# Toothbrush Connect — 3-Minute Demo Video Script

Built on `docs/Demo_ideas` (intro option 2, the "problem first" open), `docs/PRD.md` and what the app already does.
Visual flow of this script: see the [published storyboard](https://claude.ai/artifact/DGcLotdDpX8PM4yvSVrbKz) (private until

## 0. The concept: miles apart, same moon

**Theme.** Growing up means leaving home for your own adventure: college, a career, a family. The adventure is
exciting, but every mile adds distance between you and the friends who knew you first. Toothbrush Connect keeps
those friends close, and brings them along on the journey.

**Motif: "Brush me to the moon."** The app already has this built in: the brush timer is a moon that waxes from
new moon to full moon over two minutes, a toothbrush rocket orbits it, and the start screen reads *Ready for
liftoff*. The video builds its story on it:

| In the app | In the story |
| --- | --- |
| Start button: *Ready for liftoff* | Every brush is a small launch |
| Toothbrush rocket on the orbit | You, on your journey |
| Moon waxing from new to full in 2:00 | Two minutes of flight; *Full moon* means you're caught up |
| Friends brushing at the same time (Brush Buddy moment) | Your crew on board with you |
| Everyone sees the same moon | Distance doesn't matter; you're under the same sky |

**Taglines.**
- Main line (title card and close): **Miles apart. Same moon.**
- Product line (under the logo): **Fly to the moon. Bring your friends.**

**Tone.** Warm, a little wistful at the start, then uplifting. Night-time palette from the app (midnight blue,
moon white, moonbeam gold). Music: a soft jazz-lounge "moon" feel. Don't use the Sinatra recording or the song
itself, which are copyrighted; pick a royalty-free track in that style.

## 1. What makes a hackathon demo video win (research summary)

| Rule | Where it comes from | How this script applies it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Say what the app does in the first few seconds | Devpost Help Center, "Video-making best practices" | Problem lands by 0:16, product name and one-line pitch by 0:30 |
| Tell it as a story: problem → solution → demo → impact | Devpost, "6 tips for making a winning hackathon demo video" | Four acts, each with a fixed time budget, all carried by the moon journey |
| Hook in 15–20 s with an emotional moment | Nader Dabit, "How to give a killer pitch or hackathon demo" | An old photo of three friends under the moon, then an unanswered text |
| Show the product running, with real input; skip mundane flows like sign-up | Devpost; hackathon.com tips | No sign-up, no login. We open on *Ready for liftoff* and a real iMessage arriving |
| Demo only the features that support the pitch | Devpost, "How to present a successful hackathon demo" | Three features only: one-tap check-in, iMessage delivery, Brush Buddy moment |
| Benefits over features; no jargon in the demo | Dabit; TAIKAI pitch guide | Tech talk is fenced into one 30 s block after the demo |
| Script it, rehearse it, do multiple takes, edit out the mess | Devpost | Everything below is timed and worded; screen captures are pre-recorded |
| Good audio beats good video; don't speed-talk | Devpost | ~400 words of voice-over for 180 s (about 135 words per minute) |
| Answer: problem, solution, tech stack, how it meets the criteria | Devpost blog | Acts 1–4 map 1:1 to these questions |
| Upload early, check it's public | Devpost | See the production checklist in section 6 |

**Timing formula used here (from Demo_ideas):** 30 s problem and solution · 90 s product · 30 s tech · 25 s impact and next steps · 5 s close.

## 2. The three personas: one crew, three journeys

They grew up together in Columbus, Ohio, and used to sit on the same rooftop looking at the moon. Then each left
on their own adventure. Each one proves a different benefit, so the video never repeats itself.

| | **Maya, 21** — the Student | **Priya, 24** — the Young Professional | **Tom, 27** — the Busy Parent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Their adventure | College junior in Boston, first time living away from home | Consultant based in New York, three months on a project in Tokyo | ICU nurse in Chicago; his biggest adventure is his 4-year-old daughter |
| Distance from home | A state line and a lot of homework | Halfway around the world, 13 hours ahead | Close enough to drive, never enough time to |
| Why they drifted | Guilt: the group chat is 47 messages deep and she's scared to jump back in | Time zones: when she's awake, everyone else is asleep | No hands: when he's free, he's holding a kid |
| What they do in the demo | **Launches**: the full one-tap check-in | **Receives**: Maya's update reaches her in Tokyo, in iMessage | **Joins**: gets a "brushing now" ping and boards one-handed |
| Benefit it proves | Sharing takes seconds, nothing to type | Distance and time zones don't matter; updates reach you where you already are | Zero-effort, real-time togetherness |
| Colour in the video | Aqua | Pink | Yellow |

## 4. Full script

Format: **VISUAL** is what the camera or screen shows. **ON-SCREEN** is text burned into the video.
**VO** is the voice-over. **SFX** is sound. Word counts are for pacing.

### ACT 1 — HOOK, PROBLEM, SOLUTION (0:00–0:30)

**Beat 1 · Cold open · 0:00–0:06**

- **VISUAL:** A faded photo: three teenagers on a rooftop in Columbus, a big moon behind them, "summer 2019" in marker. Pull back: it's Maya's phone wallpaper. An iMessage from Priya slides over it: *"omg did you hear about Jess?? call me when you're free!!"* The "Hometown 🌙" group chat badge shows 47. Maya types *"sorry, so busy lately, will call this w—"*, pauses, deletes it all.
- **SFX:** Crickets from the photo fade into a notification ping. Keyboard clicks. Silence on the delete.
- **VO:** none.

**Beat 2 · Three journeys · 0:06–0:16**

- **VISUAL:** Map motion graphic: a pin on Columbus splits into three glowing trails that fly off to Boston, Chicago and, far across the Pacific, Tokyo. Each trail lands on a one-second live-action cut: Maya on a busy campus hears "Maya!", looks up, pockets her phone. Priya in a Tokyo office at dawn, laptop open, flips her buzzing phone face-down. Tom at a kitchen counter with a toddler on his hip, his phone buzzing out of reach.
- **VO (32 words):** "We all leave home for our own adventure. College. A career. A family. The journey is exciting, but the farther we go, the more our oldest friends drift out of view."

**Beat 3 · Title card · 0:16–0:20**

- **VISUAL:** Cut to a night sky. The moon, alone. The unread counter at the bottom ticks 47 → 48.
- **ON-SCREEN:** **MILES APART. OUT OF TOUCH?**
- **SFX:** One more ping, then the music starts.

**Beat 4 · The reveal · 0:20–0:30**

- **VISUAL:** Maya's bathroom at night, a window with the moon in it. She's brushing her teeth, phone propped on the sink showing the app's moon and toothbrush rocket: *Ready for liftoff.* Slow push-in on the phone.
- **ON-SCREEN:** "Same moon. Two minutes. Twice a day." then the Toothbrush Connect logo and **Fly to the moon. Bring your friends.**
- **VO (36 words):** "But no matter how far apart we are, we all look up at the same moon, and we all brush our teeth. Toothbrush Connect turns those two minutes into a trip to the moon, with your friends on board."

### ACT 2 — THE PRODUCT (0:30–2:00)

Screen recordings sit inside a phone frame, with the persona's name, age and location in a corner tag in their
colour (e.g. aqua tag: *Maya · 21 · student · Boston*).

**Beat 5 · Maya, liftoff · 0:30–1:00**

| Time | VISUAL (screen recording) | VO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:30–0:37 | Taps the home-screen icon. The site opens full screen: *Ready for liftoff.* She taps; the toothbrush rocket starts its orbit and the new moon begins to fill. | "Maya opens Toothbrush Connect from her home screen. It's a website, so there's nothing to install. One tap, and her toothbrush rocket lifts off." |
| 0:37–0:45 | Mood chips appear in the thumb zone. She taps **Stressful**, then *Add a line* and dictates *"midterms week, send snacks 😭"*. | "One tap shares her mood, and she can dictate a line. No typing with a toothbrush in her hand." |
| 0:45–0:52 | Taps the audience chip *Everyone ▾*, picks the **Close 3** list. Snackbar: *Sending to Close 3 in 30s · Change · Undo*. | "She chooses her crew for this flight, just her closest three, and has thirty seconds to change her mind." |
| 0:52–1:00 | Friend cards float in above the moon: Priya *FUN · this week: "Tokyo is unreal"*, Tom *BORING · today: "night shift #4"*. Jump cut (label "2 min later"): the moon is full. *Full moon. Done.* *You caught up with 3 friends.* | "As the moon fills, her friends' news comes to her. Full moon: two minutes, and she's caught up." |

*VO words: 67.*

**Beat 6 · Priya, halfway around the world · 1:00–1:20**

- **VISUAL:** Establishing shot: Tokyo skyline, morning, with a pale daytime moon (stock footage is fine). Priya in her hotel room, getting ready for work. Phone lights up. Screen recording of the real iMessage thread with Toothbrush Connect:

  ```
  [💌 JUST FOR YOU · STRESSFUL] Maya: "midterms week, send snacks 😭"
  React or share yours → tbc.link/r/a8K2
  ```

  She long-presses and sends a ❤️ tapback. Cut to Maya's screen in Boston: a heart reaction appears on her check-in. Priya taps the link; the site opens already signed in, on Maya's update. She taps **Brush now** and her own rocket lifts off.
- **ON-SCREEN:** pink tag *Priya · 24 · consultant · Tokyo, 10:30 AM*. Small caption on the iMessage: *real iMessage, sent by our agent*.
- **VO (42 words):** "Priya is thirteen time zones away and hasn't opened the app all day. She doesn't need to. Maya's update lands in iMessage, where she already is. A heart goes straight back to Boston, and one tap puts Priya on the flight too."

**Beat 7 · Tom, one hand free · 1:20–1:40**

- **VISUAL:** Tom's bathroom, bedtime. He is brushing his daughter's teeth with one hand; she's wearing rocket pyjamas. His phone on the counter shows:

  ```
  [🪥 BRUSHING NOW] Maya is brushing. Join → tbc.link/b/…
  ```

  With his free thumb he taps the link, taps *Ready for liftoff*, taps **Fun**. Show the thumb big in frame: three taps, no typing.
- **ON-SCREEN:** yellow tag *Tom · 27 · nurse & dad · Chicago*. Counter in the corner: *3 taps · 0 words typed*.
- **VO (34 words):** "Tom's biggest adventure is two feet tall, and it leaves him one free hand. A 'brushing now' text, one tap to board, one tap for his mood. He's on the flight."

**Beat 8 · Three skies, one moon · 1:40–2:00**

- **VISUAL:** Three-way split screen, one column per persona in their colour. Each column: a window with the sky and the local time on top (*Boston 9:30 PM · Chicago 8:30 PM · Tokyo 10:30 AM, next day*), the live phone screen below. All three moons fill in sync; each shows the other two friends' avatars (*Brushing with Priya & Tom*); waves and hearts fly between the columns. Then a motion-graphic transition: the three moons slide together into one big moon, with three toothbrush rockets (aqua, pink, yellow) orbiting it side by side. It fills to full.
- **ON-SCREEN:** **Three skies. One moon.**
- **VO (32 words):** "Boston, Chicago, Tokyo. Three different skies, one moon. For two minutes, the old crew is side by side again. We call it a Brush Buddy moment. Nobody had to plan a thing."

*Note for the edit:* the split screen is real app footage; the three-rockets-one-moon merge is a motion graphic
(the app shows friends as avatars on your own moon). Keep it clearly stylised.

### ACT 3 — MISSION CONTROL: HOW IT WORKS (2:00–2:30)

- **VISUAL:** Animated architecture diagram styled as a flight path, one node lighting up per phrase of the VO. Use the one in the storyboard:
  `React PWA (website)` → `Supabase: Auth, Realtime presence, Postgres + row-level security` → `30 s delivery hold (pg_cron)` → `Outbox fan-out` → `Node messaging agent` → `Photon Spectrum` → `iMessage`. A return arrow from iMessage back to the agent labelled *replies · tapbacks · STOP*.
- **ON-SCREEN:** title *Mission control*; logos along the bottom: React, TypeScript, Vite, Supabase, Postgres, Node.js, Photon Spectrum.
- **VO (76 words):** "Here's mission control. The website is a React and TypeScript app that installs like a native one. Supabase handles sign-in, live presence, and row-level security, so an update is only ever readable by the crew you picked. The thirty-second hold runs on a Postgres scheduler, then each update fans out to an outbox. Our Node messaging agent drains that outbox through Photon Spectrum into real iMessages, and handles replies, tapbacks and STOP on the way back."

### ACT 4 — IMPACT AND NEXT STEPS (2:30–2:55)

- **VISUAL:** Motion-graphics slide on the night sky. Left: the stat. Right: three roadmap chips that pop in one by one, like stars.
- **ON-SCREEN:**
  - **~24 hours a year.** *The time each of us already spends brushing.* (2 min × 2 × 365 = 1,460 min.)
  - Roadmap: **AWS** so mission control scales with every new crew · **WhatsApp & SMS** so friends on any phone can board · **Smart reminders** right before your usual liftoff.
  - North star: **4 connected days a week.**
- **VO (58 words):** "Each of us already spends about twenty-four hours a year brushing our teeth. We want that time to keep our oldest friends close, however far we go. Next, we're moving to AWS so every new crew can launch, adding WhatsApp so friends on any phone can board, and smart reminders that arrive right before your usual liftoff."

### CLOSE (2:55–3:00)

- **VISUAL:** Maya's phone. A new text from Priya: *"❤️ good luck on midterms!! 🚀"* Maya smiles. Her wallpaper, the rooftop photo, is behind the message. Cut to the full moon and the logo.
- **ON-SCREEN:** logo · **Miles apart. Same moon.** · site URL · team names.
- **VO (6 words):** "Miles apart. Same moon. Bring them along."

**Total VO: about 400 words** across roughly 165 s of spoken time, leaving silence for the cold open and title card.

## 6. Production checklist

**Before recording**

- [ ] Three accounts on three phones (Maya, Priya, Tom), all in each other's circle, with a **Close 3** list on Maya's account.
- [ ] Seed history so the feed isn't empty: Priya *FUN · this week: "Tokyo is unreal"*, Tom *BORING · today*.
- [ ] Demo mode on (migration 0007) so phone numbers verify instantly. Each phone has texted the Photon line once so it can receive.
- [ ] Agent running with `AGENT_MODE=photon` so the iMessages in beats 6 and 7 are real, not mocked.
- [ ] All three sites installed to the home screen (no browser chrome in captures).
- [ ] Phones on Do Not Disturb, full battery, clean status bar, same wallpaper (the rooftop photo). Set each phone's clock to the scripted local time for the split screen.
- [ ] Only show what works today. The weekly digest and smart reminders are not built, so they appear only on the roadmap slide.

**Recording**

- [ ] Record the VO first, in a quiet room with a real mic (or phone held close, inside a closet). Cut screen footage to the VO, not the other way round.
- [ ] Screen captures at 60 fps; scale down in the edit.
- [ ] For the 30 s hold and the 2 min timer, jump-cut with a small "30 s later" or "2 min later" label. Never pretend it's instant.
- [ ] Do at least three takes of every screen capture.

**Editing**

- [ ] Burn in captions for all VO. Many judges watch muted.
- [ ] Royalty-free jazz-lounge music under the VO at about −20 dB; none during the cold open.
- [ ] Persona colours stay consistent: aqua Maya, pink Priya, yellow Tom. The moon stays the same moon in every shot.
- [ ] Export 1080p, 16:9, under 3:00 including the logo. Check the hackathon's exact limit.
- [ ] Upload at least 3 hours before the deadline (YouTube: public or unlisted, "Not made for kids"). Open the link in a private window to confirm it plays.

**Judging criteria check** (fill in with this hackathon's rubric)

| Criterion | Where the video answers it |
| --- | --- |
| Problem / impact | Act 1 (distance and drift) and Act 4 |
| Working product / technical execution | Act 2 (real iMessages, real realtime) and Act 3 |
| Design / user experience | Beats 5 and 7 (one tap, one hand), and the moon timer as the visual idea |
| Originality | The habit-stacking insight in Beat 4 and the Brush Buddy moment in Beat 8 |
| Use of sponsor tech (Photon) | Beat 6 caption (an iMessage reaching Tokyo) and Act 3 |
